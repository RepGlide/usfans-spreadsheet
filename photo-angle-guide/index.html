Photo Angle Guide

Which photo angles are worth observing per category, and what photos still cannot prove.

Photos observe, they do not judge

A photo shows visible features. It cannot settle what is not visible. Read every image at the level it actually operates — observation, not verdict.

Angles worth seeing

  • Front — the primary face of the item.
  • Side — the profile and silhouette.
  • Back — the reverse face.
  • Detail — close-ups of specific parts.
  • Label — tags, markings, and size labels.

What photos cannot prove

  • Authenticity — a picture is not a verification.
  • Material — the look does not confirm the substance.
  • Durability — a single image says nothing about how it wears.
  • Scent — a perfume’s smell cannot be photographed.
  • Internal structure — what is inside is not shown.
  • Seller reliability — an image does not vouch for a seller.
?Open item: is any photo being read as proof of more than it shows?

A worked read

  1. Front: you observe the primary face.
  2. Detail: you see a close-up of the label.
  3. Limit: none of this proves material or authenticity.

Example read — a reading framework, not a live product check.
