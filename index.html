One Sheet, Different Questions

One Checklist Does Not Fit All

A shoe is not a perfume. Different categories need different questions, so the same fixed checklist cannot be applied to every row.

1What a fixed checklist does

  • Applies the same questions to every row.
  • Ignores what the category actually needs.
  • Produces answers that do not fit the item.

2What a category lens does

  • Identifies the product type first.
  • Picks the questions that fit that type.
  • Skips questions that do not apply.

Measurements Are Definitions

Three Kinds of Size, Three Jobs

Do not blur the three kinds of size together. Each answers a different question.

AWhat to separate

  • Label size — the size printed on the tag.
  • Page size — the dimensions the listing states.
  • Your needs — the fit or size you actually want.

BWhat not to promise

  • Do not promise a fit from a label alone.
  • Do not treat page dimensions as your measurement.
  • Do not assume one label equals your usual size.
?Open item: which of the three sizes are you actually looking at?
Photo Angles, Not Photo Verdicts

Photos Show, They Do Not Prove

A photo helps you observe visible features. It cannot settle everything about the item.

1Angles worth seeing

  • Front, side, and back views.
  • Detail and close-up shots.
  • Label and tag angles.

2What photos cannot prove

  • Authenticity and material.
  • Durability, scent, and internal structure.
  • Seller reliability.
?Open item: is any photo being read as proof of more than it shows?

Keep / Check / Stop

A Teaching Method, Not a Verdict

These labels describe how to proceed while you read. They do not describe any product’s real status.

  • Keep

    Category fits. The product type matches a lens, so you can keep reading with the right questions.

  • Check

    A detail needs checking. A measurement, option, or angle is not yet clear, so check it on the page.

  • Stop

    Out of scope. A claim goes beyond what photos or text can show, so stop and leave it open.

Teaching method label — not a real product status or QC result.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a USFans spreadsheet?

A USFans spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to research, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official USFans service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by USFans or Google.

Does the category lens provide authentication or QC?

No. The category lens is a set of check questions. It is not a store, an automated QC tool, an authentication service, or a shipping calculator. It does not provide real product status.

How do category-specific questions work?

Identify the product type first, then apply the questions that fit that category. Different categories need different measurement, option, and photo-angle questions; one fixed checklist does not fit all.

What can photos actually show?

Photos show visible features only. They cannot prove authenticity, material, durability, scent, internal structure, or seller reliability by themselves.

Where do I place an order after checking?

After you have gone through the category-specific questions and re-checked the source page, you can continue through an agent shopping service to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
